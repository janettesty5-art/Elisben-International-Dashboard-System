1st April, 2026

Dear Parents & Guardians

We give thanks to Almighty God for His faithfulness and protection over our students and the entire school community throughout the Second Term of the 2025/2026 Academic Session.

The Second Term came to a successful close on Thursday, 2nd April, 2026. We appreciate the commitment of our students, the dedication of our teachers, and the continued support of all parents and guardians.

Term Highlights

  • Successful completion of the Second Term examinations.
  • Active participation in cultural activities, particularly on Asa Day.
  • Notable improvement in students' reading culture.
  • Continued emphasis on moral and spiritual development through weekly devotion and character education classes.
