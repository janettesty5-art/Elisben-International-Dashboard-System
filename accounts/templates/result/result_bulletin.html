Elisben International Schools, Ikere Ekiti
Bulletin for the End of Second Term 2025/2026 Academic Session
and Commencement of Third Term
Date: 1st April, 2026

Dear Parents/Guardians,

We give thanks to Almighty God for His faithfulness and protection over our students and the entire school community throughout the Second Term of the 2025/2026 Academic Session.

The Second Term came to a successful close on Thursday, 2nd April, 2026.

We appreciate the commitment of our students/pupils, the dedication of our teachers, and the continued support of all parents and guardians. The term recorded remarkable academic progress, impressive performance in co-curricular activities, and good conduct among the majority of our learners.

Key Highlights of the Second Term:

  • Successful completion of the Second Term examinations.
  • Active participation in the cultural activities particularly on Asa day.
  • Notable improvement in students' reading culture.
  • Continued emphasis on moral and spiritual development through our weekly devotion and character education classes.

Vacation Period:

Students are to proceed on Second Term Vacation immediately after school hours on Thursday, 2nd April, 2026.

School resumes for Third Term on Monday, 27th April, 2026.

Important Information for Third Term:

  • Resumption Date: Monday, 27th April, 2026
  • Reporting Time: 7:20 a.m. prompt (Students must be in complete school uniform and neat appearance)
  • Third Term Duration: The term is expected to run for approximately 13 weeks.
  • School Fees: All outstanding Second Term fees must be fully paid before resumption. Third Term school fees are due for payment on or before resumption day. Late payment attracts a penalty.
  • Books and Materials: Students are expected to come with all required textbooks, notebooks, mathematical sets, and other learning materials for the new term.
  • Uniform: All students must resume in clean and complete school uniforms. No fancy wears or accessories are allowed.

Important Reminder:

  • Parents are advised to monitor their children's activities during the vacation and ensure they engage in meaningful and productive tasks.
  • We encourage continued reading and light revision of Second Term work to prepare adequately for the Third Term.
  • Any health or medical challenges should be properly treated before resumption.

We look forward to welcoming our dear students and pupils back stronger, healthier, and more determined to excel in the Third Term.

Thank you for your continued partnership in the education and upbringing of our children. Together, we shall continue to raise future leaders of excellence, integrity, and godliness.

Signed:

Proprietor

Elisben International Schools

Ikere Ekiti
