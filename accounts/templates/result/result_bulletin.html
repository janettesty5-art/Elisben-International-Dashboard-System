Dear Parents & Guardians

On behalf of the Management and Staff of Elisben International Schools, we sincerely appreciate you for your support, cooperation, and commitment throughout the 2025/2026 academic session.

This is to inform all parents, guardians, and students that the school will officially close for the Third Term and End-of-Session Vacation on Friday, 24th July, 2026, and will resume for the 2026/2027 Academic Session on Monday, 14th September, 2026.

  • Prepare adequately for the new academic session.
